The Vision

Film Belongs Outside.

I've been watching movies my whole life. In living rooms, in theatres, on laptops. And I've been convinced for just as long that the best place to watch a great film isn't inside a building — it's out here. Under a wide open sky, with the smell of a fire in the air and the sound of nature between scenes.

The Campfire is that idea made real. A curated outdoor cinema experience at Cherrywood Farm & Golf Club — where the screen is big, the sound is right, and the setting does most of the work. Come for the film. Stay for everything else.

This isn't just a movie night. It's a reason to gather. A reason to be outside. A reason to remember what it feels like to watch something that matters, with people you care about, under a sky full of stars.

— Preston Watts, Curator

"The best stories deserve the best setting."

How You'll Watch

Three Ways to Experience It

The Lawn

Spread a blanket. Set up a camp chair. Watch the Missouri sky turn from sunset gold to deep black while the screen fills with something worth seeing. This is how cinema was always meant to feel — unhurried, open, alive.

  • BYO blankets & low-back chairs
  • Premium reserved lawn seating available
  • Concessions served on-site
  • Fire pits accessible between showings

The Drive-In

Pull in. Roll down the windows. Tune to the FM frequency. Some nights call for the classic American experience — a windshield, a warm car, and a great film playing big across the field. The Campfire has you covered from behind the glass, too.

  • Dedicated drive-in parking lanes
  • FM audio broadcast to your car radio
  • Concession car service
  • Fully accessible for all mobility needs

The Fire

The credits roll. The fire stays lit. Pull up a log and let the conversation go where it goes. Some of the best film discussions happen after the movie ends — and the Cherrywood night is long enough for all of them.

  • Communal fire pits open post-screening
  • Curated discussion prompts from Preston
  • S'mores & warm drink service
  • Open until the embers say otherwise

Your Curator

Preston Watts

Preston has spent years thinking seriously about film — not just what's on screen, but why it matters, what it says, and what it does to the people watching. He brings that lens to The Campfire.

His philosophy is simple: a great film, in the right setting, watched with the right people, can change the way you see the world. The Campfire is his attempt to prove that, one screening at a time.
