⚠️Do not mow frost-covered or waterlogged turf. Raise heights during heat stress or drought — tighter cuts increase stress risk in summer.
📋
Before You Start — Every Time
1
Check the mowing schedule and your assigned area
2
Inspect equipment: blades, tire pressure, fluids, fuel
3
Set and confirm correct height for the area
4
Check turf conditions before starting
⛳
Greens
Daily, early morning before play
1
Alternate direction each day
N-S → E-W → diagonal. Prevents grain buildup and compaction.
2
Roll 2–3 times per week
Improves ball speed and surface consistency.
3
Walk the green after mowing
Check for scalping, dry spots, or early disease. Report anything unusual to the superintendent immediately.
🌿
Fairways
Morning after dew lifts, 3–4x per week
1
Follow designated mowing pattern
Consistent stripes matter for appearance and playability.
2
Overlap passes slightly — no uncut strips
3
Clear clippings if accumulation affects ball roll
🌾
Roughs
1–2x per week
1
Clean transitions — fairway/rough edges must be sharp
2
Trim around trees, bunkers, cart paths with line trimmer
🔧
Post-Mowing
1
Clean equipment after every use
2
Dull blades? Report for sharpening
Dull blades tear grass instead of cutting. Don't wait for visible damage.
3
Log: completion, conditions observed, any issues
⛳Golfer nearby? Stop, wait, make eye contact and wave before proceeding. Never start equipment near a golfer mid-swing.
