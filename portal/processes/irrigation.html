🌱 Spring
Reduce frequency as temperatures climb. Watch for dry spots as turf wakes up.
☀️ Summer
Increase frequency. Monitor greens daily during heat waves. Hand-water stressed areas.
🍂 Fall
Gradually reduce as temps drop. Prepare for winterization before first hard frost.
❄️ Winterizing
Shut off water source → drain all lines → blow out every zone with compressed air → document date.
⚠️A system not properly winterized will have broken heads and lines come spring. Do not skip the compressed air step.
