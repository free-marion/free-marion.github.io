Chemical Safety
1
Read the SDS before using any chemical
2
Wear required PPE — gloves, eye protection, boots
3
Never mix chemicals without written instructions
4
Store in designated locked areas only
5
Dispose of containers per the label
⚖️Missouri pesticide applications must be performed by or under direct supervision of a licensed applicator.
🚨
If Someone Is Injured
1
Make sure the area is safe before approaching
2
Administer first aid if trained and appropriate
3
Serious injury? Call 911 first
4
Notify the manager immediately
5
Complete Incident Report Form within 24 hours
Even for near-misses. File with the manager.
