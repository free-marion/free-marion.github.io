Daily Attendant Checklist

Clubhouse Staff
🌅 Morning — Before First Customer
All carts inspected — damage, trash, tires, operational
Carts staged neatly in designated area
Sand bottles and ball washers filled
All keys stored inside — none pre-assigned
Hole 1 tee area clear and ready
Scorecard supply stocked at counter
Registration sheet in place for today
Tee time log reviewed — notes from manager?
Register drawer confirmed at $100
Square POS operational and logged in
Phone charged and working
Bathrooms checked — stocked and clean
First aid kit visible and accessible
⛳ Every Incoming Customer
Signed in on registration sheet
9 or 18 holes confirmed
Payment in Square BEFORE cart key given
Cart number assigned and logged
Scorecard offered
Hole 1 start and pace reminder given
🔄 Every Cart Return
Marked returned on registration sheet
Trash removed and cart wiped down
Sand bottle and ball washer checked
Lost items: customer contacted, item in Lost & Found
🌙 Closing
All carts returned and accounted for
All carts wiped down, trash removed
Carts stored in shed
All keys returned to designated hooks
Problem carts tagged "Out of Service"
Square POS reconciled for the day
Drawer counted, drop slip completed
Drawer reset to $100 for tomorrow
Cash drop made to safe
Tee time log complete — no-shows marked
Trash emptied in all areas
Bathrooms restocked for tomorrow
All lights off, all doors and sheds locked
