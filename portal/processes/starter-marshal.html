Starter & Marshal Duties

Outside Staff
⛳ Starter
  • Gets groups organized at the tee
  • Verifies tee times against the log
  • Confirms cart numbers match
  • Gives rules briefing
  • Sends groups off on time
🚗 Marshal
  • Patrols the course once play is in motion
  • Monitors pace of play
  • Enforces cart rules
  • Handles slow play diplomatically
  • Reports incidents to the manager
💡One person assigned? Start groups first, then transition to marshal patrol once the course is in motion. Neither scheduled? The clubhouse attendant covers both.
S
Starter — Before Your Shift
Arrive 30 minutes before first tee time
1
Pull the Tee Time Log from the clubhouse
2
Note: group sizes, cart assignments, any special conditions
Cart path only? Hole closures? Know before the first group arrives.
S
Sending Groups Off
1
Confirm name and time from the log
2
Verify: player count (max 4), holes, cart number
3
Give the brief
"Keep pace — 2 hours for 9, 4 for 18. Start on Hole 1. Cart rule today is [___]."
4
Send off at scheduled time, 10 minutes between groups
⚠️Group missing 5–10 minutes past their time? Let the next ready group go. Try the phone number once. Update the log and tell the clubhouse.
M
Marshal — Course Patrol
Start once first 2–3 groups are on course
1
Full loop every 45–60 minutes
Watch: pace of play, cart path compliance, safety issues, course damage.
2
One hole behind — remind them
"Hey folks — you're about a hole behind. Let's pick it up if we can." Suggest skipping a hole if needed.
3
Faster group waiting — offer play-through
"Would you mind letting the group behind you play through? Keeps things moving for everyone."
💡Always friendly, never confrontational. You can be firm without being rude. If someone pushes back hard, note their cart number and radio the manager — don't escalate yourself.
