Taking a Phone Reservation
1
Get the details
Name, phone number, number of players, 9 or 18 holes, preferred time.
2
Check the log
Find the next open slot at least 10 minutes after the last booking.
3
Confirm back
"You're set — [X] players at [time] for [9/18] holes. Please arrive 15 minutes early to check in."
4
Record in the log
Name, player count, holes, time. Done.
🚶
Walk-Ins
1
Find the next open slot
Don't squeeze them in front of an existing reservation.
2
Pair singles and twosomes
When it makes sense — keeps cart efficiency up and pace smoother for everyone.
3
Log as "WALK-IN"
Note it in the comments column.

Pace Monitoring (No Marshal)

1
Visual check every 30–45 minutes
From the clubhouse or a quick drive out.
2
One hole behind = polite reminder
Go out or radio them. Document in the daily log.
