Pro Shop Closing

Closing Staff
📋Complete in order. Do not leave until every section is done.
🍎 1. Food & Inventory
All displayed food items returned to proper storage
Perishables checked — anything past date discarded
Shelves restocked for tomorrow (if supplies available)
Low-stock items added to restock list
💵 2. Register & Financials
Drawer counted down
Daily drop slip completed and signed
Drawer reset to $100 (small bills preferred)
Cash drop made to safe
🧹 3. Cleaning & Organization
PM cleaning checklist completed (posted at counter)
Display merchandise returned to correct positions
Counter and POS terminal wiped down
🔑 4. Carts & Keys
All carts in the shed — count matches the log
All cart keys on designated hooks
Nothing left in any cart (trash, tees, balls, scorecards)
Problem carts tagged "Out of Service"
🔒 5. Facility Lockdown
All shed doors closed and latched
Bathrooms, storage rooms, and back areas checked
Every door locked
Every light off
⚠️Find something unusual — unlocked door, lights that shouldn't be on, unfamiliar vehicle? Note it and call the manager before leaving.
