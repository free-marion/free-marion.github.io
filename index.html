Our Story

Tucked into the rolling Ozark foothills of Fredericktown, Missouri, Cherrywood Farm & Golf Club is one of those rare places that earns the word destination. Nine holes of golf laid down in 1948, still playing true on bent grass greens. A working farm — cattle, chickens, goats, sheep, ducks, and honeybees — all raised with intention. Come for a round. Breathe easier.

Dale Cooper started Cherrywood with a piece of land and a desire to create something meaningful. A farm came first; regenerative agriculture, cattle, chickens, sheep, ducks, even honeybees! A vision for a sustainable community, designed to connect people with nature, each other, and their Creator. Then came a need for Dale's waterslide restoration company to find a headquarters. The neighboring golf course came up for sale, and the pieces clicked into place.

Cherrywood Farm & Golf Club is named for Dale's true passion, his wife Cherry. Their 12 children and (for now) 10 grandchildren are their proudest legacy. They hope you will stop by for a round of golf or simply take a peaceful stroll in the meadow. Just watch out for the goats, they are very annoying.
