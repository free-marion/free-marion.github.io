Our Story

A One-of-a-Kind
Missouri Experience

Cherrywood is something you don't find every day — a working farm and a beautiful 9-hole golf course living side by side in the heart of Fredericktown, Missouri.

Whether you're here to walk the fairways, enjoy the scenery, or connect with the land, Cherrywood offers a relaxed, genuine experience rooted in the rhythm of rural Missouri.
